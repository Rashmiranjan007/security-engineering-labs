# Framework Mapping Notes

This project maintains an indicative crosswalk to organize controls. It is not a legal interpretation, certification, audit opinion or one-to-one equivalence.

| Framework | Primary use in this project | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| NIST AI RMF 1.0 | Govern, Map, Measure, Manage risk activities | Voluntary risk-management framework; tailor to context |
| ISO/IEC 42001:2023 | AI management system, leadership, planning, operation, evaluation and improvement | Certification requires formal scope, implementation and independent assessment |
| EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) | Role- and risk-based legal obligations for providers, deployers and other actors | Applicability and dates depend on role, system and provisions; check current official consolidated text and guidance |
| ISO/IEC 27001:2022 | Information security management and control environment | General ISMS standard, not an AI-specific certification |
| OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications | Technical risk taxonomy for LLM applications | Technical guidance; not a regulation or complete AI governance framework |
| GDPR | Personal-data processing obligations where territorial and material scope applies | Requires legal/privacy analysis of actual processing and roles |

## Mapping governance

- Maintain a mapping owner and version date.
- Record the exact source edition and clause/requirement in a production crosswalk.
- Assess applicability before declaring a control gap.
- Keep evidence tied to the system, environment, control owner and review period.
- Revalidate mappings when standards, legislation or regulator guidance changes.
