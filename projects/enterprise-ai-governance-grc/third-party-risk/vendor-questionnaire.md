# Third-Party AI Supplier Due-Diligence Questionnaire

Record supplier, product, model/version, service locations, subprocessors, assessment date, reviewer and contract reference. Request evidence proportionate to risk; do not treat a questionnaire answer as proof.

1. **Service and accountability:** What AI models and subprocessors are used? Who is provider, deployer and data processor/controller for each relevant activity?
2. **Data use:** Are prompts, files, retrieval content or outputs used for training or service improvement? Can the customer opt out? What are retention, deletion, residency and backup practices?
3. **Security:** Describe tenant isolation, encryption, key management, privileged access, vulnerability management, secure development and independent testing.
4. **Identity and access:** Does the service support SSO, MFA, SCIM, role-based access, service identities and audit logs?
5. **Model and supply chain:** How are model versions, artifacts, dependencies, datasets and updates sourced, verified, approved and rolled back?
6. **AI safety:** What evaluations exist for prompt injection, harmful output, bias, hallucination, data leakage, abuse and model drift? What limitations are disclosed?
7. **Human oversight:** Which actions can be autonomous? Can customers constrain tools, require approval and disable high-impact functions?
8. **Resilience:** What are availability commitments, recovery objectives, incident communications and service exit arrangements?
9. **Compliance and assurance:** Provide relevant certifications, audit reports, penetration-test summaries, privacy terms and AI governance documentation. State scope and exclusions.
10. **Contractual protections:** Are audit rights, breach notification, subprocessor notice, data return/deletion, liability and regulatory cooperation addressed?
